*The settings do not sync over between mobile and desktop. You will have to repeat these processes on each platform you decide to use Nuvio on—feel free to customize it to your liking, since there are some features I left out.*
- If you choose to create another profile, use the Nuvio website. There you can transfer your entire settings to another profile, as well as API keys/integrations.
## Tracking
1. Create an account for [Simkl](https://simkl.com/) and link it to Nuvio by going to Settings>Account>Tracking. 
2. Then set both your continue watching and library sources to Simkl.
3. Set your anime ID preference to TVDb.

![Nuvio Simkl](<./Attachments/Nuvio Simkl.png>)

## Layout
1. Enable AMOLED Black—Optional.
2.  Switch Desktop Navigation to Top Bar—Preference, desktop only.

![Nuvio Display](<./Attachments/Nuvio Display.png>)

### >Home Layout
1. Disable Show Catalog Type, and Enable Hide Unreleased Content.

![Nuvio Home Layout 1](<./Attachments/Nuvio Home Layout 1.png>)

2. Click the Hero Catalogs dropdown, and toggle on both Simkl Trending Movies & Shows—only two can be selected.

![Nuvio Home Layout 2](<./Attachments/Nuvio Home Layout 2.png>)

### >Continue Watching
1. Scroll to the bottom, select sort order and choose Separate Upcoming Row.

![Nuvio Sort Order](<./Attachments/Nuvio Sort Order.png>)

### >Poster Card Style (Optional, your preference)
1. Set Width to Comfort, and corner radius to Sharp.
2. Scroll down and Enable depth effect.
	1. Keep the edge glow as Subtle.
	2. Turn off Top sheen.
	3. Change Edge coverage as Half only.

![Nuvio Poster Cards](<./Attachments/Nuvio Poster Cards.png>)

### >Streams
1. Select Fusion bade URLs, paste the link below and save it—you can preview how they will look before saving.
```
https://xperience-app.com/badges/e6b834b0-3f7f-4917-9b5b-e1cd7fdccb54.json
```
2. Under Display, for Background choose Cinematic.
3. Turn on Addon logo.

![Nuvio Streams](<./Attachments/Nuvio Streams.png>)

### >Detail Page
1. For Background Mode, select Cinematic.

![Nuvio Detail Page](<./Attachments/Nuvio Detail Page.png>)

## Content & Discovery
*Most of this—except one setting—process can be found on the addons section of this guide.*
1. Disable Recent Searches.

![Nuvio Search](<./Attachments/Nuvio Search.png>)

## Playback
*This section has auto play settings you can optionally toggle on if you would like.*
1. In the Subtitle and Audio section, set Preferred Audio Language, Secondary Audio Language, and Preferred Language—the first three options—all to English.
2. Turn on both Use Forced Subtitles, and Show Only Preferred Languages.
	1. Below under subtitle rendering, you can tweak how you want your subtitles to look.

![Nuvio Subtitles](<./Attachments/Nuvio Subtitles.png>)

3. Under Stream Selection, enable Reuse Last Link.

![Nuvio Reuse Link](<./Attachments/Nuvio Reuse Link.png>)

4. Scroll down make sure both Prefer Binge Group, and Reuse Binge Group are enabled.

![Nuvio Binge Group](<./Attachments/Nuvio Binge Group.png>)

## Integrations
Go to Settings > Integrations to add your developer API keys:

![Nuvio Integrations](<./Attachments/Nuvio Integrations.png>)

### TMDB Enrichment (Required for Mobile)
1. Input your personal [TMDB API key](https://www.themoviedb.org/settings/api) and click save.
2. Then toggle it on.

![Nuvio TMDB 1](<./Attachments/Nuvio TMDB 1.png>)

3. Scroll down and toggle off: Artwork, Episodes, and Season Posters—Release dates should already be off, keep it off.

![Nuvio TMDB 2](<./Attachments/Nuvio TMDB 2.png>)

### MDBList Ratings
1. Input your personal MDBList API key and click save.
2. Then toggle it on.

![Nuvio MDBList 1](<./Attachments/Nuvio MDBList 1.png>)

3. Scroll down and toggle off: Metacritic, Letterboxd, and Audience Score.

![Nuvio MDBList 2](<./Attachments/Nuvio MDBList 2.png>)

### Connected Services (TorBox)
1. Open connected services (inside of integrations).
2. Connect/link your TorBox account.
3. Toggle on Resolve playable links, and Prepare links.

![Nuvio Connected Services](<./Attachments/Nuvio Connected Services.png>)
