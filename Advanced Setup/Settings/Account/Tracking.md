 Using Simkl for tracking your movies/shows/anime on Nuvio makes your history more accessible in the future. If you might need to switch media apps, or use other streaming services, Simkl can keep track of it all and transfer it if needed.
	- Simkl offers free PRO membership as long as you log in to site at least once a day for 20 days out of the past 30 days. *(You will NOT automatically have it after the 20 days, you MUST enable it under free PRO on their website for it to begin)*
	- With PRO unlocked you will be able to get recommendations based on your ratings/history and gain the ability to make custom lists that you can then use on Nuvio.
	- If you would like to purchase VIP you can receive 50% off the first year if you use my referral.
## 1. Accounts
- Sign up for Simkl and link your account to Nuvio by going to Settings>Account>Tracking.
## 2. Sources
- Set "Library Source" to Simkl.
- Set "Watch Progress" to Simkl.
- Set "More Like This Source" to Simkl.
## 3. Simkl Features
- Set "Anime ID Preference" to Prefer TVDB.
