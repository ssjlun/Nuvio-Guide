## Subtitle and Audio (Recommended)
- Set "Preferred Audio Language", "Secondary Audio Language", and "Preferred Language"—the first three options—all to English.
- Turn on both "Use Forced Subtitles", and "Show Only Preferred Languages".
## Subtitle Renderings
- Your Preference, you can tweak how you want your subtitles to look here.
## Stream Selection (Recommended)
- Enable "Reuse Last Link".
	- This setting auto-plays your last working stream instead of waiting for a new one to be cached.
## Stream Auto-Play
- If you do not want to choose through the list of streams manually, and instead want the first one to be chosen automatically: change "Auto Stream Selection" to the "Auto-play first source" option.
## Next Episode (Recommended)
- Enable both "Prefer Binge Group (Next Episode)" and "Reuse Binge Group".
	- Note: If you would like the next episode to auto-play: enable "Auto-play Next Episode" and set the "Threshold Percentage" to 100%.
