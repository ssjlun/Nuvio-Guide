- All of the features in the "Layout" section of settings are **completely your preference and optional to enable.**
	- Note: Not every setting from this section is included here. Only the features I suggest you turn on, are listed in this guide.
---
## Display
- Enable "AMOLED Black"
---
## Home
### Home Layout
- Disable "Show Catalog Type"
- Enable "Hide Unreleased Content"
#### Hero Catalogs
- Click the "Hero Catalogs" dropdown, here is where you can choose which catalogs are used to be cycled on the top of your home page—only two can be selected. 
	- I recommend turning on any trending movie + show catalogs as your two but you can choose any you like.
### Continue Watching
- Here you can enable to blur unwatched episode thumbnails if you would like.
#### Sort Order
- Scroll to the bottom, select sort order and choose "Separate Upcoming Row"
	- With this enabled, shows you are caught up on will appear in a separate row when expecting a new episode within the next 7 days.
### Poster Card Style
- Many features are not listed here but you can customize the size and effects on your posters in this section.
#### Custom Poster Source (Recommended)
- Uses an external service to provide posters to your library too, not just your home page.
	- Posters+ offers the best customization (as well as support for landscape posters), while BetterPosters is easiest to configure.
---
## Streams
### Fusion Style
#### Fusion Badge URLs (Recommended)
 - Select Fusion badge URLs, paste the link and save it—you can preview how they will look before saving.
	 - You can find badges on xperience. Sign up for an account and navigate to badges on the left to choose which to copy. *(You can also find +1000 custom avatars here that you can copy and apply to your Nuvio profile)*
---
## Detail Page
- Here you can enable to blur unwatched episode thumbnails (inside of a series) if you would like.
### Appearance
#### Shuffle
- Enables episode shuffle feature from any series.
	- New shuffle button appears when clicking three dots on a tv series/anime to bring up randomizer.
