a free community-made add-on for Nuvio that aggregate live broadcasts and 24/7 sports networks. (Optional)
## Setup
- Visit one of the sports streams instances. Choose any one. (there are three incase one goes down)
	- [Instance 1](https://sportsfree-us2.highfly.to/configure)
	- [Instance 2](https://sportsfree.highfly.to/configure)
	- [Instance 3](https://sports.highfly.to/configure)
- Select Live matches only, and choose which sports/leagues you would like.
- Scroll down and copy your manifest json URL.
## Install to Nuvio
- Open Nuvio, navigate to Settings>General>Content & Discovery>Addons.
- Paste the manifest json URL and click add addon.
