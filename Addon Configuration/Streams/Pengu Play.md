a free HTTP streaming addon for media centers like Nuvio that delivers high-definition and 4K video streams.
- I recommend using Pengu Play as a backup to when Torbox through AIOStreams experiences downtime. (Optional)
## Create an Account and Copy URL
- Visit [PenguPlay](https://pengu.uk/) and connect your google account.
- Scroll down and click Copy Addon URL.
## Install to Nuvio
- Open Nuvio, navigate to Settings>General>Content & Discovery>Addons.
- Paste the manifest json URL and click add addon.
	- You can toggle the addon off for now and switch it on when experiencing issues with AIOStreams.
